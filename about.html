Introduction

University Institute of Technology, Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (UIT-RGPV), is an engineering institute in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh.

The institute provides an academic environment where students learn engineering fundamentals, develop technical skills and participate in co-curricular activities.

Our Vision

To encourage quality education, innovation, research and responsible use of technology.

Our Mission

  • Build strong technical and problem-solving skills.
  • Promote teamwork and communication.
  • Encourage practical learning and creativity.
  • Prepare students for professional challenges.
